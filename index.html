Drone flying in North West England

This is a local, beginner-friendly guide to flying drones around Bolton, the West Pennine Moors and Greater Manchester. It's written by a young drone pilot from Edgeworth.

Locations

Rivington Pike & Winter Hill

Moorland near Horwich with the Pike tower and the Winter Hill transmitter mast.

Jumbles Country Park

A reservoir and country park between Bolton and Edgeworth.

Entwistle & Wayoh Reservoirs

Reservoirs near Edgeworth with woodland and the Entwistle dam.

Darwen Tower & Moor

The landmark tower on Darwen Moor, at the north edge of the West Pennine Moors.

Smithills Estate

A large estate of moorland and farmland on the north edge of Bolton.

Hollingworth Lake

A lake and country park near Littleborough in Rochdale.
