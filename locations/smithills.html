Smithills Estate

A large estate of moorland and farmland on the north edge of Bolton.

🎥 Footage coming soon: Smithills Estate

Things to think about

Part of the estate is run by a conservation charity that has its own drone policy. Check it before you fly. Watch out for livestock and walkers.

Before you fly here

  • Check the airspace with an official tool such as NATS Drone Assist / the drone safety map. Greater Manchester has controlled airspace and restricted zones, including those around Manchester Airport.
  • Get the landowner's permission and check any local by-laws or site rules.
  • Follow the Drone Code, and keep the right distance from people, buildings and roads.
  • Respect people's privacy, and don't disturb wildlife or livestock.

Disclaimer: This is an independent guide written by a hobby drone pilot. It is general information, not legal advice, and it isn't connected to the CAA. Rules change, so always check the official CAA drone pages, airspace tools and landowner rules before you fly.
